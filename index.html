Who is Ximilnet?

Hello, I'm Ximilnet.

I'm a passionate homelab enthusiast and privacy advocate. I created this platform to share my journey in building self-hosted infrastructure, managing NAS systems, and exploring the endless possibilities of open-source software.

In a world where our data is constantly harvested, I believe that you should own your data. Through my YouTube tutorials and my Wiki, I aim to demystify complex topics like Proxmox, Arch Linux, and Docker, making them accessible to everyone — from beginners to advanced users.

When I'm not tinkering with servers or writing documentation, I'm probably drinking coffee and planning my next deep-dive video.
